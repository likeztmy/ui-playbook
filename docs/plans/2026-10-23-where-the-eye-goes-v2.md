# Where the eye goes, v2

The reader already knows "bold headings, group fields". v2 teaches what the eye does that people don't expect: it predicts instead of reading. It stops at what looks like an end, skips what looks like an ad, misses changes outside its focus, and sorts by spacing before it reads a word.
