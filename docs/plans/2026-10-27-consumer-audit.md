# Why the playbook reads as 中台: an audit

Screens checked on 2026-10-27 at 1280 wide: home, `/components`, `/components/switch`, and
`/principles/attention-wears-off-waiting-doesnt`. Reference measurements are in
`docs/research/2026-10-27-dialkit-josh-puckett.md`. The playful-site study is in
`docs/research/2026-10-27-playful-references.md`.

## What makes it feel like a dashboard

1. **The card grid on the home page.** Each card has a title bar, a hairline, a white preview well
   and a description strip, with a count at top right. That is the anatomy of an admin
   widget. Josh's home has a list: a name in black, one line of grey.
2. **One face, one ink, one weight.** Everything is Geist in near-black, ranked by size alone:
   40 → 24 → 16. Nothing has a voice. Josh ranks by ink (near-black titles, `#525252` text) and
   gives the titles a serif.
3. **Demo frames are bordered white cards** with a ring and a shadow, labelled above. Each one looks
   like a form panel. Josh's figures are flat `#fafafa` wells with the caption inside.
4. **Controls are of two families.** In one Tweak panel a capsule segmented control sits above a
   10px-radius slider, and labels change size between controls. DialKit uses one row height, one
   radius and one alpha ladder.
5. **Personality appears once and leaves.** The concentric rings behind the home title are the
   only expressive move. Nothing on a principle page is ours alone: no illustration, no voice in
   the chrome, no moment of reward.

## What is right and must survive

- A sourced argument on every page, and live demos that you can touch.
- One column with one left edge and one right edge (done today).
- Restraint is the subject: motion short, delight rare, colour meaning one thing. A playful layer
  has to obey the pages it sits on.
