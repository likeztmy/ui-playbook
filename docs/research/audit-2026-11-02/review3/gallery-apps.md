## Gaps in the playbook

- Badge says never truncate; the Track departure ("the first may shorten") and Table's "truncate the secondary ones" pull the other way for label pills in a narrow row. Worth one line on the Badge or Table page: a label that doesn't fit becomes a count, not an ellipsis.
- No rule covers exported files (relative dates in a CSV that outlives the session).
- Nothing says a look's secondary fill must differ from the surfaces it sits on; Primer's `--fill-control` equals its `--bg`, and the face (D4) and the marketing buttons each hit it separately. A check over every look ("control fill vs bg/surface/sunken") would catch both.
