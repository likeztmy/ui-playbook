# Mail

A mail client. Invent 60 messages across folders.

- Folders: **Inbox**, **Starred**, **Sent**, **Drafts**, **Archive**, **Trash**.
- A message list and a reading view.
- **Compose**: To, Subject and body, with **Send** and discard. Reply and forward from a message.
- Archive, delete, star and mark unread, for one message or several.
- **Search** across all mail.
- New mail arrives every 40 seconds or so; the person is told when it does.
