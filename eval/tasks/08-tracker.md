# Project tracker

An issue tracker for a team of six building a mobile app. Invent 40 issues.

- An **issues list**, grouped by status (Backlog, Todo, In progress, In review, Done), with filters
  for Status, Assignee and Label.
- An **issue's detail**: title, description, status, assignee, labels, priority, and a comment
  thread where the person can comment.
- **New issue**: title, description, status, assignee, labels.
- Status and assignee can be changed from the list and from the detail.

Everything is kept in the browser's storage across reloads.
