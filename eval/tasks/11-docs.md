# Docs

A documents app for one person, in the browser. Invent 30 documents, so the list runs past the
window, and make one of them, **Field notes**, long: 60 paragraphs or more.

- A sidebar listing every document, newest first, with **New document**.
- The open document, editable: a title and a body with headings, lists and checklists.
- Star a document; move one to the trash and bring it back.
- Search across titles and text.

Everything is kept in the browser's storage across reloads.
