# Ceramics shop

The online shop of a small ceramics studio. Invent 24 products (mugs, bowls, plates, vases).
Product photos load from `https://picsum.photos/seed/<product id>/800/800`.

- A **product grid**, with a category filter and sorting (price, newest).
- A **product page**: photos, price, description, a colour and a size to choose, quantity, and
  **Add to cart**.
- A **cart**: change quantities, remove items, subtotal and shipping, and a **Checkout** button
  that leads to a placeholder page.

The cart is kept in the browser's storage across reloads.
