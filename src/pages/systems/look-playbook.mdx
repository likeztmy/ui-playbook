## When to use

When the brief names no product to feel like and no brand. Blue is the default; another palette changes the accent and what follows from it (its hover, its soft fill, its text), while the surfaces, lines, type, corners and motion stay. Pick one when the brief's brand already leans that way, not to make the screen more interesting: the accent is for the few things someone has to notice ([Grey until it matters](/principles/grey-until-it-matters)).

## Values

Each text colour's note gives its lowest contrast in each theme, against the four surfaces and `accent-soft` laid on each (a status colour also on its own soft fill). Text holds 4.5:1; `line-strong` and the focus ring, 3:1.

## Paste

Blue, light and dark. Every variable a component on this site reads is here, so swapping in another look changes these values and nothing else.
