## The rule

**Inner radius = outer radius − inset − border.** When both arcs share a centre, the gap at the corner is as wide as the gap along the sides. Copying the outer radius inward makes the inner shape look swollen at its corners; making it square makes it look pasted on.

<ConcentricTweak client:visible />

When the inset is as large as the outer radius, the formula reaches zero. Don't go square: floor it at `radius-xs` (4).

## Precision's pairings

The radius scale steps in 2–4px so the common insets always land on a token.

| Outer | Inset | Inner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Toolbar `radius-xl` 16 | `space-2` 8 | Tool `radius-md` 8 |
| Menu `radius-lg` 12 | `space-1` 4 | Item `radius-md` 8 |
| Segmented track `radius-md` 8 | `space-0-5` 2 | Thumb `radius-sm` 6 |
| Field `radius-md` 8 | `space-1` 4 + 1px border | Key 3 |

## Common mistakes

- **Uneven inset.** Concentric corners need the same inset on both edges that meet at the corner. A toolbar padded 4 × 12 can't be concentric.
- **One radius for everything.** A single global radius guarantees every nested pair is wrong.
