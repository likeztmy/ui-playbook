Three directions on the same menu: disabled, checkbox, submenu and destructive items, with modifier shortcuts. Each row shows a light still, a dark still, and the live menu. Try the keyboard in the live one.

## A · Native

After macOS. 24px rows at 13px, no icons, a reserved checkmark column, and a solid accent highlight that reverses the whole row. Delete reverses out of danger instead. Shortcut symbols come from the system font, and the key letters align in their own column.

<NativeDirection client:visible />

## B · Keycaps

After Linear. 32px rows at 13px with icons, a `fill-active` highlight you can find from the keyboard, and one cap per key. Modifier glyphs are Lucide icons, so the caps share one stroke. No group labels: the separators already say it.

<CapsDirection client:visible />

## C · Command

After Raycast's action panel. Type to narrow the actions; labels name the groups, since filtering mixes them. The highlight is an accent tint with a blue icon, and the keys are bare glyphs.
