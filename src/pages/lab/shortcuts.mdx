The menu used to draw a box around every key: fourteen boxes in seven rows, louder than the labels they sit beside. Apple's menus, shadcn/ui and Radix Themes all show shortcuts as bare symbols. Three directions on the same Edit menu, with one-, two- and three-key shortcuts. Rows, icons and highlight are unchanged. Hover the stills to move the highlight.

**Picked: A.** The Menu component now draws shortcuts this way, one step lighter still: `ink-disabled`, the grey macOS gives shortcuts.

## A · Column

After macOS. Bare symbols in `ink-muted`. The last key sits in a fixed cell at the right edge and the modifiers line up to its left, so Z, C, D and G fall in one column whatever comes before them. Modifiers follow Apple's order: ⌃ ⌥ ⇧ ⌘.

## B · Text

After shadcn/ui and Radix Themes. The whole shortcut as one run of symbols, a size smaller than the label, right-aligned. Simplest; the keys don't form a column.

## C · Chip

The same run on one borderless chip in `fill-hover`, darker on the highlighted row. Keeps a hint of “this is a key” without a box per key.
