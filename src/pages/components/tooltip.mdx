## Anatomy

| Part | Token | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ground and text | `ink` on `bg`, inverse | |
| Radius | `radius-sm` | 6 |
| Padding | `space-1` × `space-2` | 4 × 8 |
| Text | label size, 12 / 16, weight 500 | 12 |
| Offset from trigger | `space-1` | 4 |
| Delay | `delay-tooltip` | 500ms |
| Layer | `z-tooltip` | 400 |

One line: the control's name, then its shortcut as glyphs, dimmed. It sits above the trigger and flips below when there's no room.

## Common mistakes

- **Anything clickable inside.** A tooltip vanishes when the pointer leaves the trigger. Links, buttons or forms belong in a Popover.
- **Tooltips on labelled buttons.** A button that says *Save* doesn't need a tooltip saying *Save*.
- **Sentences.** If it needs more than a name and a shortcut, the control needs a visible label instead.

## Copy

- The tooltip's name is the control's `aria-label`, word for word: **Zoom out**, not *Zoom out of the canvas*.
- Sentence case, no full stop. Shortcut after the name: **Redo** ⌘⇧Z.
