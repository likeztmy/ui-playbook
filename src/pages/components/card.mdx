## When to use

A card is one unit in a set people browse by sight: projects, samples, templates, files. A frame holds a grey stage for the content, and the words sit underneath. Things compared by attributes are a Table; one block of a page is a section with a heading; a card whose stage is a generic icon is a list row in disguise; and cards don't go inside cards.

## Anatomy

| Part | Token | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Frame | `surface`, `shadow-xs`, `radius-xl` | 18 |
| Inset | `space-2`, all four sides | 8 |
| Stage | `fill-control`, `radius-lg` = 18 − 8 | 10 |
| Title | 14 / 20, weight 500 | 14 |
| Subtitle | 13 / 18, `ink-muted` | 13 |
| Meta padding | `space-3` above, `space-2` sides | 12 |
| Hover | `hover-lift`, `shadow-sm`, `ease-spring-gentle` | 1.01 |
| Entrance | `stagger` apart, at most eight | 30ms |

<CardAnatomy client:visible />

The stage gives the content a quiet grey; the card itself brings no colour. Colour on a page of cards comes from the work on them.

## States

<CardStates client:visible />

The whole card is one target: its title is the link or button, stretched over the card, so a screen reader hears one name. The More button sits above it and opens its own menu without opening the card. Tab reaches the card, then its action.

## Common mistakes

- **Buttons all over the card.** One primary target and at most one action. More belongs in the action's menu.
- **Text on the stage.** Titles over images lose contrast on half the images; keep words in the meta row.
- **Uneven heights.** Two-line titles in one card and one-line in the next make a ragged grid. Truncate to one line; show the rest in a tooltip or the opened view.
- **Staggering every re-render.** The entrance plays when the grid first appears, not when a filter changes or a card is renamed.
- **The stage copying the card's corner.** With an inset, the same radius swells the gap at the corners; subtract the inset.

## Copy

- Title: the thing's own name: **Q4 roadmap**, not *Project: Q4 roadmap*.
- Subtitle: the one fact that helps choose between cards, usually when it changed: **Edited 2 hours ago**.
