## When to use

A radio group picks exactly one of a few options, all in sight, and the choice waits for the form to be sent. More than five want a Select; a choice that switches a view at once is a Segmented control; options that combine are Checkboxes; a single on/off is a Checkbox or a Switch.

## Common mistakes

- **No default.** Nothing chosen is a state the user can't get back to. Choose the safe option, or add *None* as a radio.
- **A radio that acts on the spot.** If choosing changes the view at once, it's a Segmented control.
- **Yes and No as radios.** A single Checkbox says the same in one row: **Email me a receipt**.
- **Hiding the choice in a Select.** Three options with prices read better all in sight than one at a time.

## Copy

- Group label: what is being chosen: **Shipping**, **Export at**.
- Option labels: parallel, short, sentence case, no full stop: **Your team**, **Anyone with the link**.
- Description: what the choice means, not a restatement: **Including guests, once they sign in.**
