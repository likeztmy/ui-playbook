## When to use

For text people type: a name, an address, a search, a number with a unit. Reach for something else when:

- **The answer is one of a few known values.** Two to four: a segmented control or radios. Five to fifteen: a Select. More, or searchable: a combobox.
- **The answer is on or off.** A Switch or a Checkbox.
- **The value is a position on a range.** A Slider, often paired with a small number field.

- **Leading end: what the field is for.** A magnifier on a search. Nothing on a plain field: the label already says it.
- **Trailing end: what the field offers.** A unit, a shortcut key, a clear button. One at most.

## Anatomy

| Part | Token | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Height | `control-md` | 32 |
| Radius | `radius-lg` | 10 |
| Edge | `line-strong`, 1px | |
| Padding | `space-3` | 12 |
| Trailing inset | 4 from the outer edge: 1 border + 3 | 4 |
| Embedded key | 24 tall, `radius-sm` = 10 − 4 | 6 |
| Label | label, 12 / 16, weight 500, `ink-muted` | 12 |
| Label, field, hint | `space-1` apart | 4 |
| Focus | `accent` edge + 3px `accent-soft` halo | 3 |

The label sits above, the hint or the error below. Never both: the error takes the hint's line.

## Common mistakes

- **A hint that repeats the label.** *Enter your email* under **Email** says nothing. Keep the line for a format, a limit, or a consequence.
- **Errors in a toast or at the top.** Put each one under its field, where the eye already is.
- **Clearing the field on error.** Keep what they typed; they're usually one character away.
- **Read-only drawn as disabled.** Read-only text can still be selected and copied, so it keeps the ink colour.

## Copy

- Label: a noun, sentence case, no colon: **Email**, **Project name**.
- Placeholder: an example, not an instruction: **ana@studio.com**, not *Enter your email*.
- Error: what to do, with an example if the format is the problem: **Enter an email address, like ana@studio.com.** Never *Invalid input*.
