## When to use

A toast confirms that something just happened, briefly, without stopping anyone, and its most useful job is to offer Undo. A choice is a Dialog; news about one field or row belongs there; a change already plain on screen needs no toast; and anything people may need later belongs in the page, since toasts vanish.

## Kinds

<ToastKinds client:visible />

| Kind | Icon | Stays | Announced |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Done | `success` check | 4s | politely |
| With Undo | — | 6s | politely |
| Needs attention | `warning` triangle | 4s | politely |
| Failed | `danger` alert | until closed | at once (`role="alert"`) |

Hovering or focusing a toast pauses its clock; F6 moves keyboard focus into the toasts.

## Anatomy

| Part | Token | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shape | `surface-raised`, `shadow-lg`, `radius-full` | pill |
| Height | button 32 + inset 4 × 2 | 40 |
| Inset | `space-1`; `space-4` before the text | 4 |
| Width | fits the text, 320 to 440 | 320 |
| Icon | Lucide 16 in the kind's colour | 16 |
| Text | 14 / 20, `ink`, one line | 14 |
| Actions | one pill button, then Dismiss | 32 |
| Place | bottom centre, `z-toast` | 300 |

A pill in a pill: the buttons inside are pills with an even 4px inset, so they're concentric by construction.

Toasts rise 8px out of `enter-scale` on the smooth spring and leave in `duration-fast`, sinking and fading. The newest sits lowest; older ones move up, on the same spring, to make room. Three at most; a fourth pushes the oldest out.

## Common mistakes

- **Errors that disappear.** A failure people have to act on stays until they close it, and is announced at once.
- **Toasts as the only feedback.** A screen reader hears it; a sighted user looking elsewhere may not. The page itself should show the change.
- **Links and paragraphs.** One line, one action. If it needs more, it isn't a toast.
- **Toasts on page load.** Nobody has done anything yet; there's nothing to confirm.

## Copy

- Say what happened, past tense, with its object: **Deleted “Q4 roadmap”**, **Link copied**.
- Failures say what failed and why: **Couldn’t export: the file is locked**.
- The action is one verb: **Undo**, **Retry**, **View**.
