## When to use

A floating toolbar keeps a canvas's tools one click away without taking a column of the layout. Reach for something else when:

- **The controls belong to a form or a page.** Lay them out as buttons in the content.
- **The actions are rare.** Put them in a pull-down Menu; a toolbar is for what people reach for every minute.
- **The icons wouldn't be recognised.** Label the controls, or use a menu with words.

One toolbar can hold all three, each in its own group behind a separator. What changes is how pressing behaves: tools switch a mode, toggles flip their own state, actions just run.

## Anatomy

<ToolbarLive client:visible />

| Part | Token | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Panel radius | `radius-xl` | 16 |
| Panel inset | `space-2`, all four sides | 8 |
| Tool radius | `radius-md` = 16 − 8 | 8 |
| Tool size | `control-md` | 32 |
| Gap between tools | `space-1` | 4 |
| Panel height | 32 + 8 × 2 | 48 |

For a tighter panel, drop the inset to `space-1` and the panel to `radius-lg`: 12 − 4 still gives 8 for the tools.

## Common mistakes

- **Borders around groups.** Separate groups with a hairline (`line`), not boxes inside the box.
- **Two icon sets.** One set (Lucide) at one stroke. Mixing outline and filled icons, or two libraries, reads as two products bolted together.

## Copy

- Tooltips name the tool, then its shortcut: **Rectangle** R. The name matches the `aria-label` word for word.
