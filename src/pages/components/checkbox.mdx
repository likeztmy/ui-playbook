## When to use

A checkbox turns one option on or off, and the choice waits for the form to be sent: options you can combine, one option sent with a form, a parent over its children, or a bare box selecting rows. A setting that applies at once is a Switch; one of a few is a Radio group; an action is a Button.

## States

<CheckboxStates client:visible />

Pressed, the box dips to 0.9. The mark springs in from 0.4 on the smooth spring, overshooting a touch; the fill fades in `duration-fast`. Unchecking fades the mark out quickly. With reduced motion it only fades. Space toggles the focused box.

## Common mistakes

- **A checkbox that saves on the spot.** If nothing waits for a Save or Submit, it's a Switch.
- **Negative labels.** *Don't email me* turns checking into a double negative. Name what's on: **Email me about updates**.
- **Consent ticked in advance.** Marketing and data-sharing boxes start empty; checking them is the consent.
- **Disabling instead of explaining.** A greyed box with no reason is a dead end. Say why in the description, or hide it.

## Copy

- Label: what's true when it's checked, sentence case, no full stop: **Keep me signed in**.
- Group label: what the set is about: **Email me about**, **Export**.
- Description: the consequence, in a short sentence: **Anyone with the link can reply.**
