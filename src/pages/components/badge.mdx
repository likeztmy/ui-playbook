## When to use

A badge labels where something stands or what it is, in a word or two: *In review*, *Shipped*, *Beta*. It can't be clicked. Reach for something else when:

- **It does something.** A filter you can toggle is a chip or a toggle button; an action is a Button.
- **It's a count.** A number beside a tab or a nav item is a count, mono and plain.
- **It needs a sentence.** An explanation belongs in text, an inline message or a tooltip.
- **It's urgent.** A failed payment isn't a badge on a row; it's a message people have to see.

## Common mistakes

- **A badge per row in every column.** If every row has one, the column is a status column; give it a header and keep the badges.
- **Red for “new”.** Danger means something is wrong. New is `info`, or no badge at all.
- **Truncated badges.** *In revi…* says nothing. Use a shorter state, not a narrower badge.
- **Badges as buttons.** If it reacts to a click, people need a hover and a focus ring, and then it's a button.

## Copy

- One or two words, sentence case: **In review**, **Shipped**, **Beta**.
- States as adjectives or past participles: **Failed**, **Scheduled**. Not verbs: *Fail*, *Schedule*.
