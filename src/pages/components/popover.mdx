## When to use

A popover is a small panel that opens from a button and holds controls or detail about it: a short job beside its source, an explanation someone asked for, a small picker. A list of actions is a Menu; a control's name is a Tooltip; a job that mustn't be lost is a Dialog; anything that would scroll wants a panel of its own.

## Anatomy

| Part | Token | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Popup | `surface-raised`, `shadow-md`, `radius-lg` | 10 |
| Inset | `space-3`, past the radius: inner parts keep their own | 12 |
| Width | as wide as its content, at most 320 | 320 |
| Gap to trigger | 6 | 6 |
| Title | 13 / 18, 600 | 13 |
| Description | 13 / 18, `ink-muted`, `space-0-5` under the title | 2 |
| Between blocks | `space-3` | 12 |
| Actions | right-aligned, small buttons, `space-2` apart | 8 |
| Trigger while open | `fill-control-hover`; a ghost button takes `fill-control` | |

No arrow. Like a Menu, the popover grows from its trigger's corner, and the pressed trigger says where it came from.

## Common mistakes

- **Opening on hover.** A panel with controls opens on click; on hover it closes as the pointer heads for it. For a read-only preview on hover, use a hover card with a delay.
- **A popover for a decision.** If the choice can't be undone or mustn't be dismissed by a stray click, it's a Dialog.
- **Popovers inside popovers.** One level. A second panel belongs in the first, or the job belongs in a Dialog.
- **A Save that isn't needed.** A picker applies on pick; only a form with typed text needs a button.

## Copy

- Title: what the panel is for, matching its trigger: **Share “Q4 roadmap”**, **About storage**.
- Description: one sentence of consequence: **People you invite can edit.**
- Action: a verb for the job, not *OK*: **Invite**, **Apply**.
