## Anatomy

| Part | Token | Value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Height | `control-md` · `control-sm` · `control-lg` | 32 · 24 · 40 |
| Horizontal padding | `space-3` (sm: `space-2`, lg: `space-4`) | 12 |
| Radius | `radius-md` (sm: `radius-sm`) | 8 |
| Icon gap | `space-1` | 4 |
| Label | body, weight 500 | 14 / 20 |
| Edge | `shadow-xs` | 1px |

Buttons in one row share one height. Icons are 16px and inherit the label's colour.

## States

<ButtonStates client:visible />

Hover and press are overlays (`fill-hover`, `fill-active`), not darker copies of each base colour, so one rule works on every variant and in both themes. Focus shows only on `:focus-visible`: a mouse click never draws a ring.

## Details

### Hover appears at once and leaves softly

<HoverTiming client:visible />

Feedback for something the pointer is on must be immediate; feedback for something it has left can take its time. Paint the overlay on a `::before` and transition its `opacity`. A `background-image` gradient can't be transitioned, so it would snap off.

### The focus ring stacks on the edge

<FocusEdge client:visible />

When the edge is drawn with `box-shadow`, any focus style that sets `box-shadow` replaces it. List both shadows.

### One filled button per view

<OnePrimary client:visible />

The filled button is the reason the screen exists. Everything else is secondary or ghost; destructive actions use `danger` text.

## Common mistakes

- **Scaling on press.** A 0.97 scale reads as playful and shifts the label under the cursor. Switch to `fill-active` instantly instead.
- **Colour as rank.** Two secondary buttons in different colours ask the reader to learn a code. Use variants: primary, secondary, ghost.
- **Disabled with no reason.** If the action can't run yet, say why nearby, or leave the button enabled and explain on press.

## Copy

- Start with a verb that names the result: **Save changes**, **Create project**, not **OK** or **Submit**.
- Sentence case: **Create project**, not **Create Project**.
- Match the dialog's title: a dialog called *Delete project?* confirms with **Delete project**.
